Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated:

1. Who we are

Fade & Forge Barber Co. ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and the booking service available on it. Our physical address is . We are the "responsible party" for the personal information described in this policy under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

2. What information we collect

We only collect information that you give us directly. This includes:

  • Booking details — your name, phone number, email address, chosen service, chosen barber, and any notes you choose to add.
  • Contact details — if you phone, email or message us.
  • Basic technical information — such as your browser type and the pages you visit, collected anonymously through standard web hosting logs. This is not linked to you personally.

We do not collect payment card details on this website. Payments are made in person at the shop.

3. How we use your information

We use your personal information only to:

  • Confirm, manage and remind you about your appointment.
  • Contact you about changes to your booking (for example, if a barber is unavailable).
  • Respond to enquiries you send us.
  • Keep basic records for tax and accounting purposes, where required by law.

We do not use your information for marketing unless you have clearly opted in, and we never sell your information to third parties.

4. Calendar events

When you choose to add an appointment to your calendar, we generate the event on your device:

  • Google Calendar — the event details are passed to Google via a link you click. We do not receive access to your calendar.
  • Apple Calendar / Outlook (.ics download) — a calendar file is generated in your browser and downloaded directly to your device. It never passes through our servers.

In both cases, the calendar event is created by you and remains entirely under your control.

5. How we store and protect your information

Booking information you submit through this website is used in your browser to generate the confirmation and calendar event. We do not store bookings in a database on this website. Any information you share with us by phone or email is stored securely and kept only as long as needed to provide our services or to meet legal obligations.

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. Please contact us if you have any concerns.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share it only:

  • With our barbers, so they can deliver the service you booked.
  • With service providers who help us run the shop (for example, a phone or email provider), strictly for that purpose.
  • Where we are required to do so by law, a court order or a regulator.

7. Cookies and local storage

This website uses your browser's local storage to remember two small preferences:

  • Whether you prefer the dark or light theme.
  • Whether you have already dismissed the first-visit offer popup, so we do not show it again.

These values stay on your device and are not sent to us. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information.
  • Object to how we use your information.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis for processing.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in section 10.

9. Children

Our website is intended for use by adults. Bookings for children under 12 must be made by a parent or guardian, who is responsible for providing the child's information and for consenting to the service.

  • Address:

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page, with the "last updated" date shown at the top. If we make significant changes, we will make that clear on our website.
