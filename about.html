Our story

Built on the chair

Fade & Forge started with one barber, one chair, and a simple belief: a proper cut never goes out of style.

Since 2016

Old-school craft, modern standard

Fade & Forge opened its doors on Main Road in 2016. Founder Sipho Ndlovu had spent a decade cutting hair across Johannesburg and wanted a shop that felt like the barbershops he grew up in — warm, unhurried, and serious about the craft.

Today, three barbers work the floor. Every service ends the same way: a hot towel, a clean line, and a mirror you actually want to look into.
